# Rustym v0.0.7

The last UI nit from the Now Playing page: the play/pause button no longer
changes width, so nothing on the card ever reflows.

## 🔘 Fixed-width play/pause button

- The hero pill showed a short "▶ Play" when paused and a wider "❚❚ Pause"
  while playing. That width change re-wrapped the ring buttons next to it
  (like, lyrics, vinyl, visualiser, add-to-playlist, queue) on every toggle —
  reported as "the vinyl player moves when the play button changes".
- Both labels are now measured up front and the wider one reserves the pill's
  size, so the button is the exact same width in playing and paused states.
- Verified against rendered frames: with a long 3-line title, every ring
  button sits at the identical position in playing and paused states, and the
  vinyl player, card and visualiser do not move by a pixel.

## 🔧 Includes everything from v0.0.4 – v0.0.6

- Now Playing card can no longer slide off the display (wrapped hero actions,
  layout fitted from the space that really remains, unit-tested).
- Vinyl player fixed in place; visualiser exactly fills the space between the
  card and the player bar; long titles scroll instead of clipping anything.
- Lower, flat memory footprint: ~140 MB steady window-process RSS (was
  ~200 MB and growing), bounded artwork cache, freed pages returned to the
  OS, lazy CJK font loading.
- "Start minimised" works at sign-in: cold starts stay in the tray.

Build: `cargo build --release` (or grab the packages below).
